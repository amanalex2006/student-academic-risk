Reading the results

Failure recall is the fraction of failing students identified at the 50% threshold. Log loss and Brier score measure probability quality; lower is better. Subjects are evaluated separately, using the same held-out records for both input modes.

Without previous grades, failure recall is low at this threshold. These historical datasets do not establish a prospective early-warning system.
